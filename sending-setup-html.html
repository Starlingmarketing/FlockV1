Send Email Drafts

Configure settings to automatically send your Gmail draft emails.

1. Setup 2. Automation

Instructions

This tool will help you automatically send your Gmail draft emails:

Set Up Draft Emails

Make sure you already have draft emails created in your Gmail account. You can use the "Create Drafts" section of this app to generate them.

Configure Sending Schedule

Set how frequently emails are sent and during which hours of the day to optimize deliverability.

Start Automation

The automated process will open Gmail, navigate to your drafts, and send them one by one according to your schedule.

Monitor Progress

Track the sending process and make adjustments as needed. You can stop the automation at any time.

Browser Selection

Requirements

  • You need to have draft emails already created in your Gmail account
  • You'll need to log in to your Gmail account when prompted
  • Keep the browser window open during the automation process
  • Make sure you have a stable internet connection
Important:

Gmail has sending limits that vary by account. For regular Gmail accounts, the limit is typically around 500 emails per day. For Google Workspace accounts, limits are higher. Exceeding these limits may result in temporary sending restrictions.
